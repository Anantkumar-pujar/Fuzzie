# Welcome to Fuzzie

A **workflow** watches for something happening — a file changing in Google Drive
— then runs the actions you configured, like posting to Discord, Slack or Notion.

<GuideIndex />

## The four pieces

- **Connections** — your credentials for Google Drive, Discord, Slack, Notion.
- **Workflows** — one trigger plus one or more actions.
- **Publishing** — the switch that makes a workflow live.
- **Logs** — a record of every run.

<Callout variant="warn">
  **Only published workflows run.** An unpublished workflow is a draft and is
  skipped entirely when a trigger fires.
</Callout>
