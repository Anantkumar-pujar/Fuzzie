# Credits & billing

**One workflow run costs one credit** — per run, not per action. A workflow that
posts to Discord, Slack and Notion in a single run still costs one.

<StatGrid>
  <StatCard label="Free" value="10" />
  <StatCard label="Pro" value="100" />
  <StatCard label="Unlimited" value="∞" tone="green" />
</StatGrid>

Change plan on [Billing](/billing). Your balance is in the top bar on every page.

## Free

Building and editing workflows, connecting apps, publishing, and browsing logs
never cost credits. Only runs do.

## Running out

<Callout variant="warn">
  **At zero, workflows go silent.** Triggers are rejected and nothing runs — but
  no error appears in your logs, because the run never starts. If your workflows
  have gone quiet, check your balance before assuming something is broken.
</Callout>

Nothing is lost. Workflows stay published and resume as soon as you have
credits. Accounts on **Unlimited** skip the balance check entirely.
